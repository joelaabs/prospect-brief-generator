# What I sell (example: copy to my_offer.md and edit)

## Company
Independent manufacturers' rep agency. Territory: Michigan.

## Products
Industrial safety gloves (TEGERA® by Ejendals); secondary line JALAS® safety footwear.
- Cut protection, heat/welding, cold and wet, anti-vibration, chemical, disposable
- Extended sizing (some styles up to size 12)
- Sold through major industrial distributors the customer already uses (e.g. Fastenal, MSC)

## Who buys it
Safety / EHS Manager (sets the spec), Purchasing Manager or Buyer (places orders),
Plant / Operations Manager.

## Problems we solve
1. Gloves wear out too fast
2. Bulky gloves get taken off for precise work
3. Workers skip gloves because nothing fits
4. Hand cuts, burns and injuries
5. Better gloves without adding a new vendor

## Target customers
Michigan companies with steady glove use: metalworking, automotive, construction, plastics,
food processing, logistics, utilities, waste and recycling.
